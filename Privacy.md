# Confident Attempt Privacy Policy

## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Confident Attempt app for mobile devices,
together with any related services operated by Paul Schütz (collectively, the
"Application"). Paul Schütz is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

## What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application stores only the information you enter while using it. Registration is not
required. The data input by you is (a) stored on device, and (b) synchronised
and stored on iCloud, if that hasn't been disabled. A guide on how to disable
iCloud sync and storage and delete the data can be found on the app's settings
screen.

## Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your
mobile device.

## Do third parties see and/or have access to information you entered in the Application?

If iCloud synchronisation and storage has not been turned off, the information
you have input into the app might be stored by Apple on your private iCloud and
is, by default, only accessible by you.

If you turn off iCloud sync and storage and delete the data stored in iCloud,
the iCloud copy of the information you entered in the Application is deleted.
Other third parties do not have access to information you enter in the
Application.

## What are my opt-out rights?

Any data you entered in the app and stored on-device will be deleted upon deletion
/ uninstallation of the app. Data stored on iCloud must be deleted separately, a
guide for this can be found on the app's settings screen.

If you contact the Service Provider directly (for example, to report a bug or ask
a question) the information you include in your message is separate from the data
stored in the Application. You may request deletion of that message and any
related information by contacting `havhingstor.development@gmail.com`. Deletion
may be limited by the platform used to send or host the message; for example, a
GitHub issue may be subject to GitHub’s controls and policies.

## Security

If iCloud synchronisation hasn't been turned off, information is stored on
iCloud. Apple describes how it protects stored data in its
[iCloud data security overview](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651).

## Data Breach Notification

If a breach occurs involving any personal information sent directly to the
Service Provider, the Service Provider will notify you as required by applicable
law.

## Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The
Service Provider will notify you of material changes by posting the updated
Privacy Policy with an effective date. Where required by law, the Service
Provider will seek your consent to material changes before they take effect.

Previous versions of this Privacy Policy will be maintained and made available
upon request by contacting the Service Provider at
`havhingstor.development@gmail.com`.

This privacy policy is effective as of 2026-11-10

## Your Consent

If you send data directly to the Service Provider and processing is
based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting
processing carried out before withdrawal.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have
questions about the practices, please contact the Service Provider via email at
`havhingstor.development@gmail.com`.
